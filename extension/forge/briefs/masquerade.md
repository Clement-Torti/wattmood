# Masquerade: design brief

**Inspired by:** *Perfectos Mentirosos*, Alex Mírez (Spanish YA thriller/romance on Wattpad, later published).

1. **Essence:** an elite academy where everyone wears a mask and every truth is a weapon.
2. **Motifs:** the Venetian mask (the lie), cracking glass (the lie breaking), gold dust (privilege, glamour).
3. **Palette:** crimson `#c8203d` = danger, desire, exposed truth. Antique gold `#c9a45c` = wealth, status, the
   gilded cage. Base is a tinted near-black (`#0b090a`), never pure black.
4. **Type:** *Cinzel* (engraved, institutional, "old money") for titles; *Crimson Pro* for long reading;
   *Playfair Display italic* for inner thoughts and whispers.
5. **Unveiling (2.7 s):** 0–0.45 s darkness falls → 0.3–1.25 s a gold mask draws itself → 1.05 s its eyes ignite
   crimson → 1.28 s a crimson fracture splits the screen top to bottom → **1.35 s swap (fully covered)** →
   1.6–2.6 s both halves fall away with gold and crimson shards.
6. **Atmosphere:** gala lighting (crimson glow bottom-left, gold glow top-right), slow gold dust, cracks drawing
   into two corners and glinting crimson, a huge faint mask watching from the right edge, an occasional glint
   sweeping across the glass.
7. **Text reveal:** `redact`: each line arrives as a censor bar that slides away, as if secrets are being
   declassified.
8. **Motif words:** mentira/s, mentiroso/a/s, miente/n, secreto/s, verdad/es, máscara/s, plus the English
   equivalents. Underlined in crimson, with a single flicker when they appear.
9. **Moods:** Gala (midnight), Campus (daylight), Candelabra (candlelight).

Original artwork only: no cover art, logos, character names or quotations.
