WattMood

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

WattMood is a Chrome extension that changes how wattpad.com looks in your browser (skins, dark/light/warm moods, reading layout). It is not affiliated with Wattpad. We collect as little as possible, never sell data, and never use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (skin, mood, text size, language…), your reading journal (words read per day) and chapter progress. They are stored with Chrome's extension storage; settings may sync to your other devices through your own Chrome profile.
  • WattMood reads the Wattpad pages you open only to restyle them. The stories you read, your searches and your comments are never sent anywhere by WattMood.

What is sent to us

  • An anonymous random code (like WM-7Q4KX2) created when you install the extension. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Community events: “installed” and “shared” (with that code, the date and your browser language), used to compute the community points.
  • Donation pledges you choose to make: the amount and currency you typed, so we can match your Buy Me a Coffee donation. Payments themselves are handled entirely by Buy Me a Coffee under their own privacy policy; we never see your card details.
  • Coupons: when you redeem a coupon we record the coupon, your anonymous code and, if you are logged in to Wattpad, your public Wattpad username, so your unlocked skins can be restored on another device.
  • Feedback you choose to send (rating, text, the skin you were using, the extension version).

This data is stored in a private Google Sheet operated by the WattMood maintainer through Google Apps Script. It is only used to run the community goal, coupons and to improve WattMood. It is kept as long as the project runs; you can ask for deletion at any time (see below).

Fonts

Skins load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google's servers.

Children

WattMood does not ask for names, emails, ages or any contact details. Donations are optional and handled by Buy Me a Coffee; if you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before donating.

Your choices

  • Turn WattMood off at any time from its toolbar popup, or uninstall it from chrome://extensions.
  • To delete the data linked to your anonymous code or coupon, open an issue at github.com/Clement-Torti/wattmood/issues with your code (shown in the donation window), or message us via buymeacoffee.com/ctorti.
