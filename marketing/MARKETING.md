# WattMood: marketing strategy

This is a living document. Update the **Log** at the bottom after every campaign, video or partnership.

## 1. Positioning

> **WattMood makes Wattpad feel like the book you're reading.**
> Free dark, light and warm reading modes for all of Wattpad, plus skins inspired by the most-loved Wattpad books.
> New skins unlock for everyone when the community reaches each goal.

- **Hook (the feature everyone needs):** dark mode and Candlelight across the whole Wattpad website. Wattpad's web reader has no real dark mode, and the existing "Wattpad Night Reader" extension only does dark mode. WattMood starts there and goes further.
- **Identity (what gets shared):** "my book's skin". Skins are a way to signal "this is my book / my era", the same mechanism behind Spotify Wrapped and phone-theme trends.
- **Community (what makes people come back):** the points line. Everybody pushes the same line, and everybody gets the same skin when it unlocks. A donation helps everyone and, from €5, also unlocks a skin for the donor.

## 2. Audience

- **Who:** 13–24 year-old readers of Wattpad romance, YA, dark romance and "bad boy" stories.
- **Languages, in priority order:**
  1. Spanish (Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Spain): Perfectos Mentirosos, Culpa mía, A través de mi ventana, Boulevard.
  2. Portuguese (Brazil): #booktokbrasil (~15.8B views), reads the Spanish and English hits in translation.
  3. English: After, Walter Boys, Kissing Booth, Perfect Addiction.
  4. French: Morgane Moncomble, #booktokfrance, #newromance.
- **Where they talk:** TikTok (BookTok), Instagram Reels and stories, Wattpad comments and reading lists, Pinterest (aesthetics), Discord and WhatsApp group chats.
- **How they talk:**
  - by **trope** (enemies to lovers, forbidden love, step-siblings, slow burn, bad boy), not by author;
  - with **POV** formats and **aesthetic edits**;
  - with nostalgia ("Wattpad girlies", "Wattpad era").
- **Their complaints about Wattpad:**
  - ads after nearly every chapter;
  - Premium feels expensive;
  - reading at night in a white browser.

  WattMood should never promise to remove ads; it doesn't, and it shouldn't. The message is: *"at least it's beautiful and easy on your eyes"*.

## 3. The growth engine: the points line

Points: **install +10 · share +5 per day · each €1 donated +10**.

- **Goals:** each book skin has a goal. The default goals are in `extension/src/core/config.js` and can be changed live from the sheet's **Goals** tab.
- **First goal reachable within days:** Masquerade (Perfectos Mentirosos) is at 1,000 pts, about 100 installs. The first unlock is the first viral moment, so it must come early.
- **Spacing:** goals grow (2.5k, 5k, 8k, 12k…), so each unlock is a fresh event worth a video.
- **Every unlock = a content drop:** the in-extension celebration, plus a TikTok "WE DID IT" video and a pinned comment showing the next goal.
- **Donations:**
  - €5 gives a coupon that unlocks one skin of the donor's choice;
  - €10 unlocks all skins;
  - in every case the points help the whole community.

  Always frame donations as "you speed it up for everyone", never as paywalls.
- **Show the line everywhere:** in the extension (bottom-left), on the site, and in videos. Screenshot it on every update.

## 4. Author partnerships

**Rule: show each author their skin *before* its goal is reached, so they're the one who pushes it over the line.**

1. When the line is about 60–70% of the way to a skin's goal, send the author a short screen recording: the opening animation and a chapter of *their* book in *their* skin. Use the template below.
2. Ask if they'd like to share it, with no obligation. Offer:
   - an author-approved tweak (colour, symbol, closing line);
   - a personal "author's edition" coupon they can give away to their readers.
3. When they share, their readers install and the goal is reached in days. Their audience becomes WattMood's audience.
4. After the unlock, credit and thank them publicly.

**Outreach template (ES, adapt to EN/PT/FR):**

> Hola [nombre] 💛 Soy [tu nombre], creé **WattMood**, una extensión gratuita que viste Wattpad con el ambiente de cada libro.
> La comunidad está a punto de desbloquear un skin inspirado en *[libro]* (un diseño original, sin portada ni personajes). Te dejo un vídeo de cómo se ve un capítulo con él 👇
> Si te gusta, nos encantaría que lo compartieras. ¡Y si quieres cambiar algo del diseño, lo ajustamos contigo! Puedo darte cupones para regalar a tus lectores.

**Rights, read before contacting anyone:**
- Skins carry the book's title (readers recognise them instantly) but must stay clearly *fan-made, not official*, with original art: no covers, logos, character names or quotes.
- Once an author is interested, move towards an *official* skin with their written OK. That's a stronger selling point and removes legal risk.
- Some of these books are now licensed by Netflix, Prime Video, Penguin Random House or Wattpad WEBTOON Studios. Don't use film stills or the films' visual identity, and avoid "official" wording without permission.

## 5. TikTok: angles of attack

Post the same concept in ES, PT, EN and FR, with captions in the language plus on-screen text. Keep videos 7–20 s, on a trending sound, and put the hook in the first second.

| # | Angle | Format / hook | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The glow-up** | Plain white Wattpad, then a beat drop: the skin's opening animation dresses the page. "POV: you finally fixed Wattpad" | Before/after is the most shareable format, and the opening animation is made for it |
| 2 | **Which skin are you?** | 4 skins flashing, "Pick one: 🖤 Masquerade 🌧️ Rainglass 🏁 Asphalt 🌅 Dusk Avenue" | Comment bait, identity signal |
| 3 | **3 a.m. reading** | Dark room, bright white Wattpad hurting your eyes, switch to Candlelight. "Your eyes at 3 a.m. after 'one more chapter'" | Relatable, sells the core feature |
| 4 | **Trope edit** | The skin plus speech bubbles on a famous dialogue scene (no copyrighted text: use your own lines) with a trope caption: "enemies to lovers hits different in Masquerade" | Trope language is how the audience talks |
| 5 | **Community countdown** | Screen recording of the points line: "we're 312 points from unlocking the *A través de mi ventana* skin, every install counts 👀" | Shared goal, urgency, FOMO |
| 6 | **Unlock day** | Reaction to the celebration animation: "WE DID IT 😭 Rainglass is free for everyone" | Payoff, and proof the community is real |
| 7 | **Author duet / stitch** | Stitch the author sharing their skin, or duet their video | Borrowed audience |
| 8 | **Make the skin with me** | Timelapse of designing a skin: palette, symbols, font | Behind-the-scenes and transparency build trust |
| 9 | **Wattpad girlies nostalgia** | "Wattpad in 2014 vs Wattpad with WattMood in 2026" | Nostalgia trend |
| 10 | **Vote the next skin** | "Which book gets the next skin? Comment 👇" with the top comment winning | Co-creation, feeds the roadmap |

## 6. Other channels

- **Wattpad itself:**
  - a reading list "Stories that look amazing in WattMood";
  - a short story/announcement on a WattMood profile;
  - replying in book clubs.

  Never spam comments: Wattpad will ban the account.
- **Instagram Reels / Pinterest:** reuse TikToks. Pinterest suits the skin palettes and aesthetics, which are evergreen.
- **Discord / WhatsApp:** the share button produces a ready-to-paste message with the referral link.
- **Chrome Web Store:**
  - screenshots in 4 languages;
  - the first screenshot is the before/after;
  - the second is the points line;
  - title keywords: "dark mode", "Wattpad", "skins".

## 9. Suggestions to make it work

1. **Ship the skins' signature moments first.** For each book, the opening animation plus one chapter illustration is what gets filmed. Polish the 3 next-in-line skins before launch.
2. **Make sharing frictionless.** The share text already includes the referral link. Add a "share my skin" image generator later (a card with the skin and the next goal) for Instagram stories.
3. **Weekly "line update" post**, even when it moves slowly. Visible momentum brings more momentum.
4. **Let the community vote** for the next skin after the planned ones, using the Feedback tab and TikTok comments.
5. **Translate everything.** Spanish and Portuguese content will likely outperform English, because the competition is lower.
6. **Never hide reads, votes or ads.** WattMood respects authors and Wattpad, which is your best argument when you contact authors (and Wattpad).
7. **Prepare for success:**
   - a Chrome Web Store listing in 4 languages;
   - fast replies to reviews;
   - a FAQ for "is it safe?" (parents will ask).
8. **Think about Wattpad's reaction.** "WattMood" contains "Watt". Stay clearly "not affiliated", never use Wattpad's logo, and be ready to rename if asked.
9. **Minors:** never push donations aggressively. The donation window already says to ask a parent. Phrase calls to action as community actions ("install", "share"), not purchases.
